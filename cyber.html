cyber://tools

Offline text, file, cryptography and identifier utilities. Everything stays in your browser.

Text encoder / decoder

Base64, URL encoding and hexadecimal are reversible encodings, not encryption.

Text hashes

Hashes are one-way fingerprints. They cannot be decrypted. SHA-1 and MD5 are legacy and should not protect passwords.

File ↔ Base64

Works with any local file, including images. Image data URLs are previewed locally; other files can be downloaded after decoding.

AES-GCM encryption
Uses AES-256-GCM with PBKDF2-SHA-256 (250,000 iterations). Store the resulting JSON intact; it contains the salt and nonce needed for decryption.


Identifiers
